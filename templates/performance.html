Test protocol

Separate and grouped

The test split contains 15% of images. Duplicate and near-duplicate photos are kept in the same split, so a leaf cannot leak from training into testing.

Metrics

Macro and weighted

Macro scores give each of the 16 classes equal weight; weighted scores reflect class frequency. Both are reported in the downloadable classification report.

Caveat

Controlled images

PlantVillage images were captured under controlled conditions. These scores are not a promise of field performance on phone photos or complex backgrounds.
