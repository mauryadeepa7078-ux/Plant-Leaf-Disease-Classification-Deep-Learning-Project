For a better result

Photo checklist

  • ✓ One leaf, filling most of the frame
  • ✓ Soft, even daylight — avoid deep shadows
  • ✓ Hold steady so the symptoms are sharp
  • ✓ Keep the leaf against a plain background
  • ✓ Avoid screenshots, drawings or blurry photos
What happens next
  1. 1

    The image is resized to 224 × 224 RGB, exactly like training.

  2. 2

    The trained network scores all 16 classes with a real forward pass.

  3. 3

    Low confidence or poor quality produces an “uncertain” result, not a forced diagnosis.

!

Confidence is not certainty. It is the model’s score for one image, not a guarantee that the plant is diseased. Consult an agricultural expert before treatment.
